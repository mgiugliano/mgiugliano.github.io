On Podcasts

2024-01-06

← Back to Blog

While I have no TV at home and never owned one for 20+ years, I love listening to (audio) podcasts. As I wake up in the morning or while commuting/traveling, I enjoy fresh news from the BBC’s World service or latest episodes on HAM radio, Python programming, as well as Mindfulness.

Apps

There are many apps available, most of them cheap or even free (e.g. see Downcast, Snipd, Radio Public, etc.). Downcast is my favorite because of its playlist and automatic next in the list. I have also played a bit with Snipd, but could really not fit its intriguing (automatic) speech-to-text transcript in my routine.

Which Podcasts?

Here are some of the Podcasts I subscribed, spanning from news to science outreach and from technology to meditation.

  • (BBC’s) Global News Podcast

  • (NY Times’) The Daily

  • (BBC’s) The Documentary Podcast

  • (BBC’s) Ukrainecast

  • Nature podcast

  • (BBC’s) The infinite monkey cage

  • Brain stuff

  • The Joy of x

  • CodeNewbie

  • Command Line Heroes

  • Talk Python to Me

  • DitDit.fm

  • Foundations of Amateur Radio

  • QSO Today Podcast

  • Secular Buddhism

  • Ten Percent Happier

  • (BBC’s) The English we Speak

  • (The Voice of America’s) Learning English

I hope this might be of help.
