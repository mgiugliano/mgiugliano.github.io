# On Podcasts

I haven't owned a TV set for over 20 years but I have always been an avid consumer of podcast shows. As I shower in the morning or commute to work, or board a 8h flight overseas, I enjoy listening to fresh news, latest episodes on Linux, HAM radio, Python programming, or even Mindfulness.

# Apps
There are many apps available, most of them cheap or even free (e.g. see [Downcast](https://www.downcastapp.com/), Snipd, Radio Public, etc.). Downcast has been my favorite for a long time, because of its playlists management and automatic "Play next in the list". I have also explored Snipd a bit, but could really not fit its (automatic) speech-to-text transcript in my routine, despite being cool. I am currently testing the free version of [Overcast](https://overcast.fm/).
