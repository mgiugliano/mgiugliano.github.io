While I have no TV at home and never owned one for 20+ years, I love listening to (audio) podcasts. As I wake up in the morning or while commuting/traveling, I enjoy fresh news from the BBC's World service or latest episodes on HAM radio, Python programming, as well as Mindfulness.

## Apps

There are many apps available, most of them cheap or even free (e.g. see Downcast, Snipd, Radio Public, etc.). [Downcast](http://www.downcastapp.com) is my favorite because of its *playlist* and automatic *next in the list*.
I have also played a bit with Snipd, but could really not fit its intriguing (automatic) speech-to-text transcript in my routine.
